Adhan recordings used for prayer-time alerts

  This adapted recording is shared under the same CC BY-SA 4.0 licence.

adhan-fajr.mp3 (also android/app/src/main/res/raw/adhan_fajr.mp3)
  Source: "Eid al-Fitr Fajr azan at Malmö Mosque - 19 August 2012.webm"
  https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Eid_al-Fitr_Fajr_azan_at_Malm%C3%B6_Mosque_-_19_August_2012.webm
  Author: Islamic Center Malmö
  Licence: Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 (CC BY 3.0)
  https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/
  Changes: audio extracted, trimmed to the adhan itself (salawat after the
  adhan removed), mono MP3 96 kbps, loudness-normalised, short fades.

Both recordings were checked phrase by phrase against the standard adhan
wording (the Fajr recording includes "as-salatu khayrun minan-nawm" twice).
